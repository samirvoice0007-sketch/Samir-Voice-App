When you open or interact with the Bot, we automatically receive certain information from Telegram, and you may provide additional information while using specific features:

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate core features of the Bot, including tasks, check-ins, referrals, and the leaderboard.

  • Process withdrawal requests to the wallet address you provide.
  • Display your name, username, and profile photo on public leaderboards within the app.
  • Communicate with you regarding your account, rewards, or support requests.
  • Improve the reliability, security, and functionality of the Service.
